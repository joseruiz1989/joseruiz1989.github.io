I'm Jose Eduardo Ruiz Rosero, a Ph.D. Engineer with over 14 years of multidisciplinary experience (including +5 as postdoctoral researcher) at the intersection of Machine Learning, Semiconductor Physics, and Software Engineering.

My journey has taken me across four continents — from growing up in Colombia (UDENAR), earning my M.Sc. and Ph.D. at PUC-Rio (Brazil), conducting research at Fraunhofer ISE (Germany), and working in applied AI roles across Latin America.

I specialize in building intelligent systems that bridge cutting-edge research with real-world deployment — from transformer models for oil reservoir simulation to quantum photodetector optimization using genetic algorithms.
