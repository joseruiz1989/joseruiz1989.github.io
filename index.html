I'm José Eduardo Ruiz Rosero, a Ph.D. Engineer with over 14 years of multidisciplinary experience at the intersection of Machine Learning, Semiconductor Physics, and Software Engineering.

I specialize in building intelligent systems that bridge cutting-edge research with real-world deployment — from transformer models for oil reservoir simulation to quantum photodetector optimization using genetic algorithms.
